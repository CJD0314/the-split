# THE SPLIT — RULES
Locked 28 Sep 2026 after DET–BUF, ATL–GB, NYG–LAR, LAR–DEN.

A six does not lock if any gate below is FAIL.

Showdown roster is **1 CPT + 5 FLEX = 6**. Never 7.

---

## ORDER OF WORK · PRIMETIME

1. Stamp the side in one sentence. Sim may only veto.
2. Official inactives (T-90).
3. Target tree.
4. Attempt lift.
5. 10k draw.
6. Roster gate + identity check.
7. Mean six **and** p90 six.
8. Build the six from the four-core, then last seats.
9. Ownership overlay (total / flex / CPT). Sheet is overlay, not the ticket.
10. Lock. Live kills on the page.

If a gate fails, stop. Do not invent a six to fill the page.

---

## GATE A — ROSTER

After official inactives:

1. Live DK list: every ACTIVE QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST with a salary.
2. The 10k table has **exactly one row per name**. No slashes. No `others`. No dump bucket.
3. Print on the dive, before any six: `Sheet N · Rows N · Missing N`.
4. Missing > 0 = **FAIL**. No lock. No IDENTITY PASS.
5. OUT names deleted. Their share re-splits onto remaining actives **at that position**. Do not park Higbee on Parkinson. Do not park Puka on Ferguson by default.
6. A review dash is only for a sheet projection of zero. Sheet number + no 10k row = **MISS**.

Bugs this kills:
- MNF 9/21 Ferguson missing, 6–54–1.
- TNF 9/24 28 sheet / 18 rows. Moore on the winner. Hooper on the highest legal six.
- SNF 9/27 Higbee 8–62–1 and Mumpfield 4–93–1 treated as seats.

## GATE B — TARGET TREE · BEFORE THE DRAW

For every OUT skill player write one line:

`OUT [name] · last two games [targets / yards / TD] · this week goes to [name %] [name %] [name %]`

Rules:
- Shares at that position sum to 100%.
- The replacement who exists because of the OUT is a **first-class row**, not a $2,000 seat.
- Last week’s “involved TE” is not this week’s TE unless the tree says so.
- Print the tree on the dive above the 10k table.

SNF 9/27 tree that should have shipped:
Puka + Atwell OUT → Adams + Higbee + Mumpfield + extra Kyren rec + extra Stafford attempts.
Not Adams + Ferguson 22%.

## GATE C — ATTEMPT LIFT

If a WR1 or TE1 is OUT:

- QB attempts move **up 8–15%** from the healthy base.
- Print `ATT old → ATT new` on the dive.
- Catchers take the extra volume in the same run. Do not tax the QB and leave catchers healthy (TNF Penix 176 vs ATL catchers 202).

Stafford 33 att with Puka out two weeks is a process fail before a single draw.

## GATE D — TWO SIXES

The 10k page prints both:

| Six | What it is | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **MEAN six** | Highest mean-DK legal row | Cash / 200–400 field |
| **P90 six** | Highest p90-DK legal row | Take-down / 1k+ field |

Do not lock the mean six into a 14k and call it leverage.
Do not lock a unique 10-point captain and call it a take-down.

Last night: mean six was Waddle + Ferguson. P90 six was Stafford + Adams + Kyren + Nix + Mumpfield/Higbee. We bet the mean. First place was the p90.

## GATE E — ROLE VS COMMITTEE

A running back with a listed partner cannot show 40+ rush attempts as a **mean** unless last-week snap share was ≥ 65%.

Harvey 43 rush mean / 2 actual carries. That mean cannot captain a take-down.

## GATE F — SCORING TAIL

The draw includes, at thin rates:

- Pick-6 / defensive TD (~3–5%).
- 4-FG script when red-zone TD rate is poor.
- 2-point tries when a team is chasing.

Or stop using DST / K **means** to fade Mevis and a home DST.
Mevis 5.3 vs 16 and Broncos DST 6.2 vs 14 were missing events, not bad luck.

---

## GATE G — MEAN IS NOT A LOCK

A player with mean DK ≥ 12 is a **must** only if:

- ≥ 25% of **this week’s** target tree, **or**
- p(DK ≥ 20) ≥ 18% in the 10k.

Else they are a dart. Ferguson 12.0 and Waddle 17.9 were means we promoted.

Fade candidate, not lock: any 45%+ FLEX name who is **not** in the four-core.

---

## 10K IDENTITY CHECK · STILL REQUIRED

After the run, before paint:

1. Home QB pass yards = that team’s WR + TE + RB rec yards. Away the same. Gap > 8 = fail. Re-scale catchers to the QB.
2. QB pass TDs = team rec TDs. Gap > 0.10 = fail.
3. Rush TD and rec TD are separate columns on a back.
4. Pass-catcher priors on each side sum to 100% **on named rows**.
5. Team points ≈ 6×(pass TD + rush TD) + kicker FG points. If the score tile and skill TDs disagree, the score tile wins and TDs scale.
6. Print both identities (`Stafford 239 = LAR rec 239`).
7. Rust / Q1-exit tax on a QB hits his catchers in the same run.
8. An `others` bucket that makes the math look closed is still a fail.

Identity is necessary. It is not sufficient. A closed identity on the wrong shares still ships the wrong six.

## HOW THE SIX IS BUILT

Format: **1 CPT + 5 FLEX**. Cap $50,000. Both teams. Leaving $500–$3,000 unused is legal.

### Four-core first

If both QBs are viable and both featured skill players are viable, the row **starts** with those four (example last night: Stafford, Nix, Adams, Kyren).

Captain is whoever among the four is cheapest to 1.5× **or** the one the field is under-captaining. Captain is the last click, not the first.

A 10-point committee back is not a captain to “be unique.”

### Last two seats

1. Rank remaining actives by **this week’s target tree + 10k involvement**.
2. The OUT-replacement is in the conversation before Engram / Trautman / a $200 TE.
3. Write A vs B in one sentence of involvement. “Fits the cap” is not a sentence.
4. Salary is a filter after. Cap is a ceiling.

### Field size

| Field | Job | Six |
|---|---|---|
| ~200–400 | Cash / unique-punished | Mean six. Chalk CPT allowed if that player is in the four-core. |
| ~800–1,200 | $100 SE | Four-core + one tree replacement. CPT = under-copied name **inside** the four-core. |
| ~10k–15k $5 | Take-down | P90 six. Still four-core first. Unique is the last seat, not Harvey CPT. |

SE and GPP week pool are different products. Do not mix optimizer uniqueness into a six-man lock.

### Fade write-up

Fade of ≥ 35% FLEX or ≥ 10% CPT gets one written why. If we fade a four-core name, the why has to survive the Gibbs / Adams test.

### Legal row on the page

Always print the legal four-core + two-tree-seats row even if we submit a different six.

## AFTER THE CONTEST · 10K SCORE

Grade three things only. Carry only what repeats.

1. **QB attempts** within 8 of actual.
2. **Top 4 target-getters** match the tree (order can move; the four names cannot be wrong).
3. **Total** inside the 20th–80th band. Two straight games in the 10% tail = the total model is the problem.

Then grade the six against the winner, not against the story.

## CLASSIC GPP · SUNDAY 150

Separate product. Do not borrow showdown uniqueness.

1. A 25%+ owned RB who can print 28 is in **30–45%** of a 150. Leverage is 15–20% off the field, not 8%.
2. Do not spend 14% on names the field already faded unless p90 ≥ 20 **and** the script is real.

4. Two-TE cap stays unless the tape says otherwise.

- Stamp the side first.
- Juice fade is not an auto-plus.
- n=1 boxes stay tagged.
- Combined 10k rows hide players.
- An others bucket fakes identity.
- One ownership number is a bias.

- Mean-as-lock is a bias.
- Slow kills are a bias. QB exit is immediate.

## ENGINE · VOLUME FIRST

Showdown 10k is `showdown_10k.py`. Checklist is `SHOWDOWN-PROCESS.md`. Live page paint is `nfl-showdown.js`.

Do not run the old score-first Poisson-then-split model for a six.

1. Layer 0 written on the dive (tree, ATT lift, committee cap).
2. Layer 1 draws NegativeBinomial pass attempts + one script factor. Rush attempts are residual.
3. Layer 2 allocates targets and carries with a multinomial on the tree. Separate RZ tree.
4. Layer 3 draws Gamma YPT/YPC. Identity is automatic.
5. Shared shocks only: script, pass_eff, run_eff.

The 10k must print QB pass-att mean and p90. If p90 attempts stay under 45 with a WR1 out, Layer 0 failed.
