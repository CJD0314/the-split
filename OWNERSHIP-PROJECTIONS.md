# OWNERSHIP + PROJECTIONS
Locked 28 Sep 2026 after the Week 3 95.1k (median 73,400th).

Sheet is a map of the field. It is not the ticket. 10k / our tree is our map. The pool rate is the only number we upload.

## TWO COLUMNS, TWO JOBS

| Source | Job | Not the job |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet own + sheet proj | Where the field will be | Who we play |
| 10k mean / p90 + tree | Who we think scores | Who the field likes |

If those two agree, the name is **chalk we also want**. Rate him.
If the sheet loves him and the tree does not, he is a **fade cap**, not a zero if he is slate-RB/QB1.
If the tree/p90 loves him and the sheet is asleep, he is a **force min**. That was Geno 7% / 30 FPTS and Sadiq 4% / 26.5.

## CLASSIC 150-MAX — RATE CARD

Before the optimizer runs, every pool name gets one tag. The tag sets min/max % of 150. No tag, no upload.

| Tag | Test | Min of 150 | Max of 150 |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLATE | Our sentence of the week (this week: SEA/NYJ pass + Gibbs) | 30% | 50% |
| FORCE | Tree or p90 in our top group, sheet own ≤ 8% | 20% | 35% |
| CHALK-YES | Sheet own ≥ 20% and we also want him | 25% | 45% |
| CHALK-NO | Sheet own ≥ 20% and we do not want him | 5% | 12% |
| PUNT | Price only, no tree | 0% | 8% |
| OUT | Inactive or zero tree | 0% | 0% |

Rules:
- A name cannot be FORCE and PUNT.
- SLATE names cannot sit at 8%. Gibbs at 12/150 was a tag fail.
- FORCE names cannot sit at 0–2%. Geno 0 and Sadiq 2 were tag fails.
- CHALK-NO is a cap, not a delete. Walker-class chalk can still be 12%.
- Sheet proj does not create a tag by itself. Waddle 18 proj was not SLATE.

## WHAT WE PRINT ON THE DIVE / DFS PAGE

One line per pool name:

`NAME · sal · sheet proj · sheet own · 10k mean · 10k p90 · TAG · min-max`

If that line is missing, the optimizer does not see the name.

## AFTER THE SLATE

Three checks only:

1. Did every SLATE name land inside its min-max?
2. Did every FORCE name land inside its min-max?
3. Sheet own vs actual own on the names we tagged — if we were off by 15+ points, the map was wrong, not the tag logic.

Do not rebuild the tag table from one week of winners. Rebuild a tag when the *sentence of the week* was wrong.
