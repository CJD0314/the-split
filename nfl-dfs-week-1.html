HOW WE PLAY THESE TWO CONTESTS

$100 SINGLE ENTRY · ~2,222 ENTRIES

One lineup. You are not playing a 50/50. First place in a field this size usually needs a top-3 score, not a safe cash floor. Build one correlated lineup with a real ceiling and one piece the field will leave off.

  • Do not play Gibbs and Bijan together. That pairing is expensive and common.
  • Do not play Burrow + Chase + Higgins in the same single. That is the most copied stack in a 2,222 field.
  • Best single shape: shootout QB + one of his WRs + a second-game RB who is not Gibbs.
  • If you use Gibbs, the rest of the lineup has to be cheap and different. No Chase on that build.

$5 GPP · 832,000 ENTRIES · 50 LINEUPS

First place needs a slate-breaking score. Duplication kills you. Fifty lineups is enough to cover three cores. It is not enough to smash Gibbs in 40 of them.

  • Gibbs in about 12–18 of 50. Never zero. Never 40.
  • Chase in about 12–18 of 50. Rarely with Gibbs in the same lineup.
  • Three cores: (1) CIN/TB, (2) LAC, (3) DET without Gibbs or with Gibbs + cheap unique bring-back.
  • At least 8 lineups with no Gibbs and no Chase.
  • Cap any one DST at 15 lineups. Cap Mayer at 10 — Bowers is out and Mayer will be chalk at $2,900.
