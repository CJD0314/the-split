WHAT WE GOT RIGHT
  • Cubs pen rank (23rd) was real. The card correctly flagged that a short Imanaga outing would expose the unit.
  • PCA as the HR vehicle made sense on paper: leadoff, 41 HR, Wrigley, vs a 4.41 righty.
  • Pirates form (4-1 last five) and WC pressure were accurate context.
WHAT WE GOT WRONG
  • The dog never stayed in the game. Dotel got lit; the +159 never had a live window.
  • We treated the 23rd-ranked pen as a primary edge for the underdog ML. It only mattered if the starter was short. Imanaga was not.
  • PCA HR at +320 lost clean. One hit, no extra-base power on the night.
WHAT WE MISSED
  • The blowout script. 12-2 is not a "pen comes in early and the dog hangs" game.
  • Cubs offense volume vs a depth arm. Multiple multi-run innings erased any dog path.
  • We did not write a hard "if the favorite starter goes 6+ this ticket is dead" clause on the ML.
HOW TO GET BETTER
  • Plus-money dogs still need a realistic path for the starter to keep it one-score into the middle innings. If that path is thin, pass or fade.
  • Pen rank is a secondary edge only. Lead with starter quality and early-inning run expectancy.
  • HR tickets at long odds still need a concrete pitch-mix or park-wind reason that is stronger than "good hitter vs mediocre arm."
