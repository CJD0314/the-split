WEEK 1 REVIEW

WHAT WE GOT RIGHT
  • Swift 18-124-3 in a 96-point game. The Wednesday 13-10 did not mean Sunday stayed under.
  • Henry 24-144-3. Featured back in a run identity was the slate.
  • Jeanty two receiving scores. New-look home back beat Miami.
  • Jefferson two TDs. The Packers side died. The Viking scored.
  • Gibbs 29-156-2. Chalk that actually scored was allowed. The cap rule still stands.
  • Bijan, Irving, Goedert, Likely all scored. Player and side are different tickets.
  • Not captaining a TE in a low total was the right rule even when we still paid Henry and Kincaid on the betting card.
WHAT WE GOT WRONG
  • McCaffrey 68 rush, 20 receiving, 0 TD. Volume was not a captain and it was not a TD ticket either.
  • Kincaid 130 yards, 0 TD. Tight-end yards are not fantasy if they do not finish.
  • Harrison 0 TD on a Cardinals side that cashed. Do not chain WR1 to a dog you already like.
  • Hunter Henry missed. Raridon scored the first Patriot TD. The cheap TE was the wrong Patriot.
  • Travis Hunter anytime missed. Dead road offenses do not give you the extra dart.
  • If a lineup still leaned Chase plus Gibbs as the whole identity, that was the field, not the 50.
