SINGLE ENTRY BUILD

Single entry is correlation plus floor. You are trying to be alive in a 150-max or a $12 single without needing a 40-point WR2.

  • The salary problem is RB. Gibbs is $8,000. Five backs cost more than every QB. If you pay Gibbs, your second RB has to be Irving $5,700 or a $6,000 band (Montgomery HOU, Swift). You cannot pay Gibbs and Bijan and still reach Chase.
  • The QB band is flat. Allen $7,000 down to Goff $6,000 is $1,000. That is the edge. Spend the extra $1,000 at RB or WR, not at QB, unless you specifically want Allen's rush floor.
  • Clean single-entry path A -- CIN game. Burrow $6,900 + Chase $7,800 + Irving $5,700 bring-back. That is $20,400 of 50.5 correlation. Remaining $29,600 for RB2, two WRs, TE, FLEX, DST. Barkley or Hampton as RB2. St. Brown or Jefferson as WR2. Jaguars $3,400 or Titans $3,100 at DST.
  • Clean single-entry path B -- DET game. Goff $6,000 + Gibbs $8,000. That is $14,000 and you still need a WR1. St. Brown $7,500 makes it $21,500 on one team. That is a GPP shape, not a single-entry shape. If Gibbs is the RB, the WR1 should be Chase or Jefferson, not a second Lion.
  • Clean single-entry path C -- PHI game. Hurts $6,600 + Barkley $6,900. Home favorite, rush floor, 44.5. Leave Smith off so you can still pay Chase or St. Brown.
  • DST in single entry is Jaguars $3,400 or Titans $3,100. Chargers $3,500 is only if the rest of the lineup is already cheap. Do not cap a single-entry build with LAC DST and Gibbs.
  • TE is a punt. Andrews $3,900 or the cheapest TE who is not a zero. Single entry is not won at TE in Week 1.
GPP BUILD

GPP is where the field will be 30-40% Gibbs and 30-40% Chase. The edge is the second piece in those games, and the stacks the field will not pay.

  • Ownership gravity. Gibbs $8,000 vs New Orleans is the most owned player on the slate. Chase in the 50.5 is next. If you play both, you need a unique third that the Gibbs/Chase field cannot fit. That third is Egbuka, Johnston, Burden, or Lawrence -- not St. Brown, who the same field also wants.
  • The $8,000 problem is also the leverage. Fading Gibbs in a GPP is live because the field cannot fade him. The replacement is Hampton $6,800 (spread) or Irving $5,700 (total) or Taylor $7,300 (workload). You are not fading running back. You are fading the one back everyone else is forced to click.
  • CIN is the GPP game. Field will play Burrow + Chase. The GPP version is Burrow + Higgins $6,300, or Mayfield + Egbuka as the other side, or Burrow + Chase + Irving so you own both teams in the 50.5. Double-game stacks in the highest total is how 150s get won in Week 1.
  • DET is the other GPP game. Field will play Gibbs without Goff. The leverage is Goff $6,000 + St. Brown $7,500 without Gibbs, or Gibbs + LaPorta without St. Brown. Saints bring-back (Olave / Johnson) only in a 149-max if Detroit is already on the slate as the favorite.
  • Cheap smash stacks the field will ignore. Lawrence $5,800 + Thomas $5,500 + Jaguars $3,400 is $14,700 of the same 39.5. That is a GPP core because it is ugly and correlated.
  • LAC pass stack vs LAC run stack. Field will play Hampton. Herbert + McConkey + Johnston is the pass version of the same favorite. Do not play Hampton and the full pass stack unless you are in a large-field GPP and want the entire Chargers side.
  • Leave NYJ/TEN skill out of GPP too. A 38.5 does not produce unique 30-point WRs. It produces 8-point DSTs. Use the salary. Do not hunt heroes there.
  • One lone Giant or Cowboy is not available on this slate. SNF is a different contest. Do not force a Sunday-night name into a 1:00-4:25 build.
