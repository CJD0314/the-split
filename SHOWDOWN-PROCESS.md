# SHOWDOWN PROCESS
Locked 28 Sep 2026. Rule method added 2 Oct 2026. Skip a line and the six does not lock.

TNF / SNF / MNF only. Classic 150 is a different product.

## RULE METHOD
A rule has four states. It moves one state at a time. It does not skip.

1. Note. One slate produced it. It is written. It cuts nothing.
2. Branch. It is on the winner in 5 of the library. It can fill a seat only after the confirmed seats are in. It cannot knock a confirmed seat off.
3. Cut. It is on the winner in 6 of the library. It can remove a player.
4. Dead. A new winner drops it below its state. It moves down one state that night. It is not argued back up.

Library today is 8 winners: NE-SEA, DET-BUF, IND-KC, NYG-LAR, ATL-GB, LAR-DEN, PHI-CHI, PIT-CLE. When a ninth winner is added, every rule is re-scored before the next lock. Six of 9 becomes the cut line. The count moves. The method does not.

A rule is one sentence and one column. "WR1 stays in" is a rule. "Be less biased" is not.

Before a note becomes a branch, write the winner it would have missed. If that winner is in the library, it stays a note.

## CONFIRMED CUTS
- A quarterback is paired with a catcher from his team. 8 of 8.
- The WR1 stays in. A catch rate does not cut him. 7 of 8.
- The cheap receiver from the tree is in the flex. A blocker is not that seat. 7 of 8.
- Captain is the WR1 or the quarterback who throws. 6 of 8.

## BRANCHES
- Both quarterbacks. 5 of 8. In if the WR1 and the cheap receiver still fit.
- Kicker or defense. 5 of 8. Name who comes off. Second tight end or lower back.
- One back from each side. Failed Buffalo and Philly. In if the cap holds.
- One tight end. Failed Buffalo. Not a cut.

## EXCEPTIONS
DET-BUF had no back and two tight ends. PHI-CHI had no back. PIT-CLE captained a back. IND-KC captained a tight end. These stay listed.

## FORMAT
1 CPT + 5 FLEX = 6. Cap $50,000. Both teams. Never 7. Salary added on the page before the six is called legal.

## CLOCK
1. Stamp the side.
2. Inactives.
3. Tree on the page.
4. 10k. Mean and p90 on the page. Neither is the ticket.
5. Confirmed seats first. Branches only if the cap holds.
6. Re-score if a new winner was added.
7. Lock.

## BUILD
1. Quarterback with a catcher.
2. WR1 in.
3. Cheap receiver in.
4. Captain is the WR1 or the quarterback, unless a listed exception is the decision.
5. Second quarterback, one back each side, kicker. Stop when the cap breaks.
6. Salary total printed.

## DOORS
Both doors at a position stay eligible until a number rejects one. Print share, catch rate, yards per target, snaps, captain ownership. A one-game collapse can reject. A season catch rate on the WR1 cannot.

## GATES
Roster complete. Tree printed. Confirmed seats filled before a branch. Kicker swap named. Salary added. New rule at 6 of the library or it does not cut.

## AFTER
Score the six against the winner. Add the winner to the library. Re-score every rule. Demote anything that dropped. Do not write a cut the same night.

## BANNED
7-man rows. Catcher without his quarterback. Cutting the WR1 on catch rate. A branch used as a cut. A note used as a cut. A rule with no column. A six whose salaries were not added.
