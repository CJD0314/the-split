SCRIPT
Most likely band: Seahawks 23–27, Patriots 17–24. One-score game. Not February’s 29–13.
  • Seattle still has the better defense and the home crowd. That is why they remain the favorite. The number moved from DK -3.5 to -3 because New England’s passing math improved when both Seattle safeties came off the sheet.
  • McDaniels should live in 11 personnel and attack Love / Finley / Thomas with Henry, Douglas, and Doubs after Brown occupies Witherspoon. Tempo after the first chunk play is how Maye stays off schedule-sack downs.
  • Fleury’s first call sheet as OC, plus Price as the lead back, is why the total never left 44.5. Early-down runs to settle the rookie, then play-action to JSN. If New England stuffs first down, Darnold becomes a dropback passer — that is Kuhr’s stated plan.
  • Will Campbell vs Seattle’s edge is the hidden game. Repeat Super Bowl pressure and Maye’s ceiling dies even with the safeties out.
  • Horton inactive concentrates Seattle targets. JSN volume is safer. Kupp is still a sit unless they live in slants vs Patriots zone.
