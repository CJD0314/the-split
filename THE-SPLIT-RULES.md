# THE SPLIT -- locked template
Source of truth: nfl-week-1-sf-lar.html as of 2026-09-09 night.
Every future dive copies that page. NFL and college football use the same breakdown. Do not invent a new layout.

Must match:
- One HOME header on a dive. REVIEW is its own page after the game is final.
- Brand, week, centered logos, full names, time / venue / network
- Four centered tiles: SPREAD / TOTAL / MONEYLINE / MOVE
- SPREAD, TOTAL, MONEYLINE each show Current: and Open: in the same large white type
- MOVE is ONLY open-to-current line movement. Examples: "Spread -6.5 to -5.5. Total 50 to 50.5." or "Held." Nothing about players, coaches, injuries, TV, or narratives.
- MONEYLINE lists the price. Write "heavy" only if the favorite is worse than -600.
- TEAM BREAKDOWN in aligned two-column rows: STAFF, WHAT THAT MEANS THIS WEEK, KEY PLAYERS, IN THE NEWS
- Staff labels: (calls plays - offense) and (calls plays - defense)
- Personnel always translated in parentheses when used
- Travel notes live in IN THE NEWS only
- INACTIVES split by team, then offense / defense. Team name once.
- SCRIPT: TEAM WINS IF -- winning score first. No base-band box
- WR VS CB split by team. Notes say who covers whom and why it matters
- College football: no DFS, no DK salaries, no lineup review
- NFL primetime only: DK salaries + lineup review
- BEST BET: pick, then $102 to win $100 (minus juice) or $100 to win $X (plus juice). No Open. No extra markets.
- After the game: REVIEW button on the home row. Correct / incorrect / missed / get better.
