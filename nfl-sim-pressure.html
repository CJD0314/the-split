SIMULATED PRESSURE

Show extra. Send four. Steal a blocker. Leave a hole. Week 1 tape on whether it worked.
THE IDEA IN ONE LINE

Show five, six, or seven at the line so the offense slides protection. Rush only four. Drop the extra bodies. You steal a blocker and still play coverage with seven.

Blitz = five or more actually run to the QB. Creeper = four rushers, one from the second level, no pre-snap show. Simulated pressure = four rushers, pre-snap show. Fire zone = five rush, drop a lineman, three-under three-deep.

WHY OFFENSES FALL FOR IT

Five-man protection cannot declare "most dangerous" when six bodies are on the ball. The line slides. The QB checks hot. The play menu shrinks to quick game. Then the defense drops the man the slide was built to block, and the rusher away from the slide walks in free.

If the QB throws the hot to the vacated hook, sim pressure loses. If he holds it because the picture still looks like Cover 0, sim pressure wins.

THE LOOKS
  • Whip — drop the edge away from the Will. Will through the B gap. Fangio staple.
  • Wasp — same family, Will through the A gap.
  • Slide-read A/B — Flores. Interior rushers read the slide after the snap. To the slide they drop. Away from the slide they rush. Five linemen block three. Free runner.
  • 70 / six-up / seven-up — Flores stacked look. Seven almost never come. The offense still checks hot.
  • Fire zone — send five, drop a lineman, 3-under 3-deep. Safer than Cover 0. Still plus-one on the protection.
WEEK 1 TAPE

Minnesota — sim that became a real blitz. Preseason they lived in show-and-drop. Week 1 they sent it: 78% of Love dropbacks, 53% pressure. Cashman untouched. Turner 7 hits. GB left the blindside free rusher. Worked until the fourth. This is stacked pressure that stopped simulating.

Philadelphia — sim that stayed sim. Fangio two-high, Cover 9 weak rotation, off-ball simulated pressures. Hunt 7 pressures, one team sack (Campbell). Daniels stayed inside a field goal. The show was there. The extra man did not finish. Commanders covered +5.5.

Dallas — sim without a rush. Parker, Fangio tree. Seventh-highest blitz rate, 21st in pressure. Four-man rush: two pressures, Dart 17-of-19 for 200 and two scores. Nickel leaked 215 yards. Showed extra. Did not steal the blocker. Giants and Likely attacked the vacated zone.

Houston — the anti-sim. Static Wide-9. Almost no disguise. Allen 7.9 yards per play. When you never show extra, the offense keeps its full menu.

HOW WE BET IT AND HOW WE PLAY DFS
  • Vs Flores stacked looks: the first question is the line's time to pressure. Inside 2.5 seconds, fade the offense and play the WR who sits in the vacated hook.
  • Vs Fangio/Parker sim: the first question is whether the four-man can finish. If not, the TE in the seam and the points are live (Likely, Goedert).
  • Vs a static no-show front: dual-threat QB keeps the full menu. That is the side.
  • DFS: the cheap TE or slot who lives in the hook the Will just vacated is the unique piece. Not the home QB on a sim-heavy favorite that cannot finish.
