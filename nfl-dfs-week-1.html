THE 50-ENTRY RULE

An 832,000-person contest is not won by the best projection sheet. It is won by a score the other 831,950 did not copy. Fifty lineups is a scalpel. If 30 of them look like the public core, you have 20 real tickets and 30 dead ones when Gibbs goes for 22 instead of 38.

HARD CAPS — DO NOT BLINK

  • Gibbs: 8 of 50. Not 18. The field is 35–45% on him. You need him if he explodes. You do not need him if he is only good.
  • Chase: 8 of 50. Same logic. When you play him, never with Gibbs.
  • Burrow + Chase: 4 of 50.
  • Herbert: 8 of 50. Best projection on the board is also the most obvious late-slate stack.
  • Mayer: 4 of 50. Bowers is out. The entire field just found a $2,900 TE.
  • Jaguars DST: 6 of 50.
  • Lineups with neither Gibbs nor Chase: at least 28 of 50.
  • If two lineups share 6 of 9 names, delete one and rebuild it from an edge below.
EDGES THE FIELD WILL MISS

1. WRONG SIDE OF THE 50.5 GAME

Everyone will stack Burrow-Chase. The surprise is Mayfield as the QB and a Bengal as the bring-back. Mayfield rushes. He is $700 cheaper. Projected ownership is about half of Burrow. If Cincinnati wins 31–28, the field’s Burrow-Chase tickets die together and Mayfield + Higgins + Irving lives.

  • Build (12 lineups): Mayfield + Higgins or Egbuka + Irving. No Bucs WR with Mayfield. One Bengal WR only.
  • Build (4 lineups): Mayfield + Chase, still no second Bucs skill player.

2. DETROIT WITHOUT GIBBS

Detroit without Gibbs is the cleanest leverage on the slate. Goff $6,000 + St. Brown $7,500 is cheaper than Gibbs alone plus a random WR2. If Gibbs gets 18 PPR and St. Brown gets 28, you pass the field.

  • Build (8 lineups): Goff + St. Brown. No Gibbs. LaPorta in 3 of these, not Mayer.
  • Build (4 lineups): Shough + Olave + Gibbs as the only Lion. That is the contrarian way to still have Gibbs.

3. ATLANTA IS A RUNNING BACK, NOT AN OFFENSE

Tua out. Penix out. Cooper Rush starts. The field will still sprinkle Drake London because the name is on the board. Fade every Falcon passer and pass-catcher. Bijan becomes a volume back in a low total. That is an edge, not a stack.

  • Bijan in 14 lineups as a one-off RB. Zero Atlanta WRs. Zero Rush.

4. THE CHEAP QB NOBODY STACKS

  • Lawrence $5,800 vs Cleveland — 6 lineups with Jaguars DST and one Jaguar skill. Low total, high uniqueness.
  • Kyler Murray $5,500 — 5 lineups as a rush QB with Jefferson or a cheap Viking, not both plus a $8,000 RB.
  • Brissett $5,100 — 4 lineups with Johnston $4,700. If LAC-ARI becomes 34–24 the other way, you own the side the field refused.
  • Lamar $6,800 — 5 lineups with Flowers, no Henry. The field pairs Lamar with Henry. You pair him with the receiver.

5. TE IS WHERE THE FIELD GETS LAZY

Mayer at $2,900 after Bowers’ surgery is the most obvious click on the slate. You beat that by paying $300–$800 more for a TE tied to a stack the field is not on.

  • Njoku $3,000 with Herbert — 6 lineups.
  • Juwan Johnson $3,700 — 8 lineups, more if Kamara sits Sunday.
  • LaPorta — 6 lineups, only in Goff builds.
  • A random $2,500 DST-adjacent TE is better than Mayer in 10 more lineups. Do not default to Las Vegas.

6. LATE-WINDOW SURPRISES

  • Jeanty $6,600 vs Miami — 7 lineups. Bowers out concentrates Las Vegas toward the back. The field still prefers Achane at $7,000 in a 40.5 total. Flip it.
  • Tre Tucker $4,000 — 6 lineups as the cheap Raider WR now that Bowers is gone.
  • Quentin Johnston $4,700 — 8 lineups. McConkey is the owned Charger. Johnston is the double-digit-target surprise if McDaniel leans vertical.
  • Egbuka $6,200 over Chase — 10 lineups. Same game, half the ownership, same shootout.
