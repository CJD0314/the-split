SCRIPT

MISSOURI WIN IF -- MIZ 34 | KU 24

  • Simmons stays clean through the first two series. Four TDs against Pine Bluff do not count. Zero turnovers in a sellout does.
  • Green holds the left side so Kansas cannot live on plus-down pressure. If Lange is in by the second drive, this script flips.

  • Olugbode wins one isolated shot against two-high. That is the play that turns 27-24 into 34-24.

KANSAS WIN IF -- KU 27 | MIZ 24

  • The sellout plus early downs. Kansas has to stay on schedule so McDonald can hunt Simmons on 2nd-and-long, not 2nd-and-4.
  • Simmons gives one away. That is the only upset path that does not require Kansas to out-talent an SEC roster for 60 minutes.
  • Pickett plays enough snaps that Missouri cannot roll coverage to the rest of the room. If he is a decoy, Kansas becomes a one-dimension run team and -5.5 is live the other way.
  • Kansas finishes one red-zone trip that Missouri did not have to defend against Pine Bluff. A field-goal night in a 50.5 total is how the dog keeps this inside a score.
