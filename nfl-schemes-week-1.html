NFL WEEK 1

DEFENSIVE SCHEMES

What they ran. What it leaked. How the next breakdown attacks it.
1. FLORES SIMULATED PRESSURE — MINNESOTA

Blitzed 82% of snaps and 78% of Love dropbacks — highest vs a QB in eight years. Love was fine for three quarters and dead in the fourth (31.6 rating vs blitz). Fifteen hits, four sacks, last 29 points.

Scheme: Simulated pressure. Force 11 personnel. Put everyone on the line once you are behind the sticks.

Hole: Early explosives. Watson 147. Love 387.

Next breakdown: Can that QB live through 40 blitzes? If yes, take the points and the WR1. If no, take Minnesota late and Jefferson.

2. FANGIO / QUARTERS — PHI, DAL, NO, SEA, GB

Two-high shell. Cover 4 / 6 / 8. Post-snap roll to Cover 3. Nickel and penny as the real base.

  • Eagles — Won 24-22, leaked 5.5. Goedert beat it in the red zone. Attack the TE, not a smash home number.
  • Cowboys (Parker) — 3-4 on 41% of snaps (112 yards), nickel on 35% (215 yards). 78% zone, 67% Cover 3 or 4. Nickel was the hole. Do not lay the brand.
  • Saints (Staley) — Cover 3 heavy, low blitz. Gibbs 156-2 through the light box.
  • Seahawks (Macdonald) — 3-4 that becomes 4-3 with Emmanwori at SAM. Three INTs. Never pulled away.
  • Packers (Gannon) — Penny, Cooper on the edge, third safety. 83% zone. Allowed the last 29.
3. WIDE-9 / STATIC ZONE — HOUSTON

Ryans / Burke. Four-down Wide-9. 80% static shells. Almost no disguise. Stunt-heavy front four.

Tape: Allen 334, 7.9 yards per play, 409 yards. First 400-yard game under Ryans since Christmas 2024.

Hole: Dual-threat QB plus play-action. No disguise to recover with.

Next breakdown: Dual-threat vs Houston — the visitor can be the side.

4. BIG NICKEL / JOKER — BALTIMORE

Minter calling it. Weaver running it. 3-3-5. Hamilton as joker. Hendrickson 58.6% pressure on field, 20% off. Four plays over 15 yards, none over 25. Jones 149 pass yards. Taylor 98-2.

Hole: Run fit. Pass D was a no-fly zone.

Next breakdown: Real back, no deep threat — run it. Live in the air — fade the pass game.

5. FOUR-DOWN PRESSURE FRONTS — CIN, JAX, PIT, LV, NYJ
  • Bengals — Lawrence lets them play bigger and still rush four. Four takeaways, defensive TD. Front is the identity.
  • Jaguars — Five sacks, 1-of-8 third down.
  • Steelers — Watt pick-six. Ugly 20-13. Next home under 44: the front.
  • Raiders — Five sacks plus Jeanty.
  • Jets — Glenn 2-4-5 nickel. 195 yards, 38:46 TOP.
